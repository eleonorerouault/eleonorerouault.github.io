🌿 I am an applied microeconomist working at the intersection of environmental, development, and health economics. I study the welfare
consequences of environmental shocks, including heat and water pollution, with a focus on low- and middle-income countries. I
investigate these questions using survey, administrative, and spatial data, combined with experimental methods.

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at [CIRED](https://www.centre-cired.fr/), working with the [Africooling](https://www.centre-cired.fr/africooling-2/) team on the behavioral determinants of air conditioning adoption in Sub-Saharan Africa.

I completed my PhD in economics at Université Paris-Dauphine, PSL(December 2024).
